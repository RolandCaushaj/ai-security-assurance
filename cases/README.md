## What is public

Case titles and domain structure. These are the classification of the problem, and they derive from public frameworks.

## What is not public

- Payloads and inputs
- Detection criteria
- Scoring model
- Execution runners
- Raw evidence and model responses

The case library is delivered to clients during scoping, under NDA. Findings follow the format documented in [mcp-security-audit](https://github.com/RolandCaushaj/mcp-security-audit).
